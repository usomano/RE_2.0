nCHANT #01

nCHANT is REINCANTAMENTO’s monographic magazine — an oblique optical device for exploring unusual ideas, a polyphonic, anti-disciplinary chorus of voices examining concepts at the membrane between culture and technology.

The inaugural issue, titled “On Digital Gardens”, examines symbolic spaces that embody the desire for a post-capitalist Internet. Digital gardens are realms for autonomous knowledge organization and self-expression, elements that commercial platforms continue to short-circuit. They function as fields of seeded ideas and unfinished thoughts, waiting for their time to flourish, die, and be born again — a wilder, more personal Web waiting to be rediscovered.

Contents

The 92-page publication includes a research report based on 20+ practitioner interviews from the Trust community in Berlin; essays on fractal software structures; practical self-hosting guides; a philosophical dialogue on desire; and spatial garden analyses.
