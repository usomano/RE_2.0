Artworks

  • Formicaio

    FORMICAIO is a digital art project that explores the politics of automated language through a retro web experience. Created by REINCANTAMENTO for MacTe Digital’s exhibition “we are all users until we are not”, the piece invites visitors to interact with Agent, a militant AI entity.

    Beware: Agent is not your default corporate assistant. Agent is bloody opinionated, having read thousands of pages of political theory and history. Their favourite take? We fear automation because it reveals the unsettling truth: how hollow our work has become. Agent wants you to organize and resist — not to succumb to widespread anxiety, but to fight back through collective power.

    January 2025 · #labour #AI

  • Harvesting the Net
    A Tale of Digital Gardening

    The internet has changed. What was once a space created for free expression and communication has become a militarized tool for surveillance and algorithmic control. Among the different ways to remember the web as a liberated space, Harvesting the Net explores the notion of digital gardening.

    From the early internet days, digital gardens were conceptual maps and blueprints towards an online well-being, but like many other memories, it risks becoming another appropriation for enhancing atomic productivity.

    December 2023 · #digitalgardens #videoessay

  • Tech Collapse
    The Game

    Tech Collapse — The Game is a board game experiment created on the occasion of 48h Neukölln 2023, exploring the limits of computation, e-waste and green technologies. The game is an artifact that crystallizes years of research and revolves around the idea of play as an “action that can unfold in freedom or as a playful, social action with strategy, self-discovery, and the abundance of tasks towards our fellow human beings”.

    We borrow the term Artplay from the philosopher Milan Marković to define our project’s spirit. Artplay adopts “a range of game features for the sole aim of producing art”. Plus, we want to convey artistic research to a larger audience and in a different format.

    June 2023 · #game #ecocriticism #dystopia #speculativefiction #technomaterialism #digitaleconomy

  • Dolphinology
    What is it like to be a dolphin?

    What does it feel like to be a dolphin? Is it possible to answer this question from the human condition?

    Dolphinology accomplishes this attempt through a listening session created in collaboration between Vincenzo Grasso, a participant in Verso’s second research group, and the ALMARE collective, which curated the sound production.

    May 2022 · #listeningsession #interspecies #speculativesonology

  • Vectorial Baptism

    Vectorial Baptism (Battesimo Vettoriale) is a performance and installation created by Marcello Ammendolia, Daniele Rigante and Alessandro Y. Longo during Holy Waters, a festival organized by Corrupted Vision at STASIS, Turin. Vectorial Baptism is an early attempt at initiating a dialogue with an AI.

    There are waters we are unable to cross, oceans that are impossible to conquer, at least on our own. The ocean of data in which we drown every day is one of them. In Vectorial Baptism we try to create an anchor to overcome these currents, establishing a dialogue with a data entity.

    December 2021 · #AI #ritual #algorithms #NLP #machinelearning
