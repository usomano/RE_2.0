Dolphinology

Dolphinology
What is it like to be a dolphin?

May 2022 · #listeningsession #interspecies #speculativesonology

What does it feel like to be a dolphin? Is it possible to answer this question from the human condition?

Dolphinology accomplishes this attempt through a listening session created in collaboration between Vincenzo Grasso, a participant in Verso’s second research group, and the ALMARE collective, which curated the sound production.

Concept
Vincenzo Grasso
Sound production
ALMARE Collective
Language
The piece is in Italian

A guided meditation

Dolphinology takes the tone of a guided meditation by mixing it with imaginative practice, and leads the audience into the sound world of the dolphin, where the primacy of the senses is not entrusted to sight and the greatest threat is precisely noise pollution.

An attempt to capture experiences of the world foreclosed to us through proximity and knowledge of other life forms — a listening session that takes the form of an imaginative practice guided into the sound world of the dolphin.
